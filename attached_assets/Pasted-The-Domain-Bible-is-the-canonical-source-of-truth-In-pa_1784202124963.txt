=========================================================
OBJECTIVE
=========================================================

Wallet-to-Wallet Transfer must remain a first-class Transfer transaction throughout its entire lifecycle.

Editing a transfer must NEVER convert it into a normal expense or income.

The transfer identity must remain intact.

=========================================================
CURRENT PROBLEM
=========================================================

After the TransactionEntryForm migration:

- existing transfers open as if they were normal transactions
- destination wallet information is lost
- saving the form recreates the transaction without transfer metadata
- editing a transfer corrupts the transfer itself

This is a regression introduced by the unfinished migration.

=========================================================
REQUIRED BEHAVIOR
=========================================================

When opening an existing transfer:

- preserve transaction.type == transfer
- load source wallet
- load destination wallet
- load amount
- load date/time
- load notes
- load every transfer-specific property

The form must recognize that it is editing a Transfer.

=========================================================
WHEN SAVING
=========================================================

Do NOT delete the transfer and recreate it as a generic transaction.

Preserve the Transfer identity.

The save operation must behave as a Transfer update, not an Expense update.

All transfer metadata must survive editing.
