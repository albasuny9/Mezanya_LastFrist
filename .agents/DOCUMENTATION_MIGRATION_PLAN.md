## Migrations explicitly NOT proposed

- No migration is proposed for anything under `docs/architecture/Mezanya Domain Bible/` — the Bible is the destination for migrations, never a source, and is never to be edited without direct authorization regardless.
- No migration is proposed for any ADR — ADRs are immutable decision records; if a decision changes, a new ADR supersedes the old one, it is not edited in place.
- No migration is proposed for `docs/project/investigations/*.md` — these are point-in-time forensic records and should remain as-is even after their findings are acted on.

## Sequencing note

Items #1 and #2 (the two Money Distribution/Location memory files) are the highest-value migrations but are **blocked** on an architectural decision (ADR-0004) that this documentation-governance work does not have the authority to make. Item #3 (filling the empty Financial Engine chapter) likely depends on the same resolution. Items #5 and #11 are the only P1 items with no blocking dependency and could be actioned first if/when migration work is approved.
